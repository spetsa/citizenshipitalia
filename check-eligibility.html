Who qualifies for Italian citizenship by descent in 2026?

Short answer: if you were born outside Italy and hold another citizenship, you can usually be recognized only if a parent or grandparent held exclusively Italian citizenship, or a parent lived in Italy for two continuous years as a citizen before your birth. Cases started by 11:59 PM Rome time on March 27, 2025 follow the old rules.

Your situationLikely result
Parent or grandparent who held only Italian citizenship at your birth (or at their earlier death)Likely eligible; the consulate or court route depends on your line
Application, appointment or court case started by March 27, 2025Assessed under the pre-2025 rules
Line passes through a woman whose child was born before 1948Court case; must also meet the 2025 rules (1948 cases)
Italian-born grandparent who became a U.S. citizen before you were bornGenerally not eligible under current rules; check reacquisition if they are alive
Claim relies only on a great-grandparent or earlier ancestorNot eligible for new applications today; follow EU Court case C-816/26
