Editorial Standards

How we write and review our guides

Our guides are written by the Citizenship Italia team for American families, in plain English. Aldo Ponte, our Head of Citizenship Procurement, who holds a law degree from the University of Turin, reviews every guide for accuracy, and each guide shows his name and the date of the review.

  • Sources you can check. Every guide links to its sources: Italian law on Normattiva, the Foreign Ministry and consulate websites, and court decisions.
  • Dated and updated. Each guide shows when it was last updated. When the law, a court ruling or consulate practice changes, we update the guides it affects.
  • Information, not a promise. Guides explain the rules; they are not legal advice for your case, and we never guarantee outcomes. Your free consultation applies the rules to your family's documents.
  • Corrections. If you spot an error, email team@citizenshipitalia.com and we will check it.
